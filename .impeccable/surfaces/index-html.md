# Surface: index.html (home)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Matvey's professional network arriving from LinkedIn/links; job: grasp who he is in a minute, verify the record, get in touch or grab the CV.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A career drawn as a transit network. Lines are what Matvey does (go-to-market, ads & measurement, build & AI, people & research); stations are where he did it. Refuses the dark SaaS hero with gradient text, glow blobs and a stat strip.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white map paper, ink black, four saturated line colours (signal red, transit blue, line green, ochre) at full strength as strokes, never as gradients. Stations are white ticks and black-ringed interchange circles. Type: Barlow (signage-born grotesk) for everything, condensed cuts for station labels. Square-ish 4px radii, 45° line geometry, no shadows except the "you are here" marker.

STORY: Visitor sees one network that explains the career at a glance, understands the four lines, believes the proof numbers pinned at each station, then downloads the CV or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: name, line legend doubling as nav, Download CV. Headline set large across the top left, one-line lead, Email + CV actions directly under it. The full-width network map fills the lower 60% of the viewport: Exeter → UCL → Echobox → Cloudflare → Google, London to Dublin, ending in a "You are here" headshot marker. Each station carries its role and one proof number.

FORM: Transit network diagram; position 1 of my ordered list (the user picked my top candidate over the dealt #7). seed:dd4e632ce4d5a30c. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a line in the legend isolates that line across the map and the page; stations link to their entries. Motion grammar: lines draw in once on load (stroke-dashoffset, ease-out), nothing else animates by default.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
