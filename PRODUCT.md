# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Matvey's professional network in general: colleagues, peers, past managers and LinkedIn visitors who land on the site from a profile link, an email signature or a shared link. They want a credible, memorable answer to "who is this person and what does he do" in under a minute, and an easy way to get in touch or grab the CV.

## Product Purpose
A single-page personal CV site (matveylutsenko.com). Success: a visitor remembers Matvey as "the commercial strategist who builds the systems and tools", can verify the track record quickly, and knows how to reach him.

## Positioning
A go-to-market/commercial strategist (Google, Cloudflare, Echobox) who, unusually, builds things himself: AI diagnostic workflows, experimentation frameworks, outreach systems and a working Chrome extension (Tag & Consent Auditor). Anthropology training (UCL, Exeter) informs how he studies the way people actually work before designing a process.

## Operating Context
Static site on Cloudflare, auto-deployed from `main`. Visitors arrive on desktop and mobile, often from LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- One static `index.html` with inline CSS/JS, no build step.
- Must keep: CV download (`Matvey_Lutsenko_CV_2026.pdf`), email, LinkedIn, GitHub links, SEO/OG/JSON-LD metadata.
- Content may be tightened and de-duplicated; no new factual claims may be invented.

## Evidence on Hand
- Stats: 143% avg. quota attainment (Cloudflare), 90% solution adoption across a 50-business portfolio, 82% pitch coverage, 100.2% revenue run rate (Google), 4× Top Outbound BDR, EMEA BDR of the Quarter Q3–Q4 2024.
- Project: Tag & Consent Auditor, Chrome extension prototype with screenshots `tag-auditor-dark.jpg`, `tag-auditor-light.jpg`; code at github.com/StarlessKnave/tag-consent-auditor.
- `headshot.jpg`, `og-image.jpg`.
- No testimonials, logos-permission or press exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work and the numbers rather than adjectives.
2. Builder first, salesperson second: the systems are the story.
3. Fast to scan: one minute should be enough.
4. Honest: every claim traces back to the CV.
